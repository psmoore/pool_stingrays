Practice Schedules

All five pools on one calendar. Use the Facilities menu to pick a pool — or leave it on All to see the whole team at once.

Filter to your swimmer’s group with the chips under the calendar. It opens as a list — five pools in one week grid is denser than anyone needs — and Week, Day and Month are a tap away. Where a group name repeats across sites, the chip says which site: King George Manta Blue, Massad Manta Blue.

What changed

Before

Five buttons, five PDFs. To answer “where is Silver on Thursday?” you opened the right one, found the right block, and hoped it was the current version. Nothing on the page said when any of them was last changed.

JRSSC Massad YMCA Ron Rosner YMCA King George YMCA Caroline YMCA

After

One calendar. The pool menu offers exactly these five — not every pool in the system — and All shows the team across all of them at once, which no PDF could do.
